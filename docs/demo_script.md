# 5–10 Minute Demo Script

1. **Login (30 sec)** — Sign in as Sales Rep.
2. **Dashboard (45 sec)** — Explain total leads, high-priority leads, campaigns and simulated activity.
3. **Leads (60 sec)** — Search and filter by industry, company size, title, status, score and signal type.
4. **High-score lead (75 sec)** — Open a lead and show scoring components.
5. **Why Now (60 sec)** — Explain the stored growth/hiring signal, recommended action and message angle.
6. **AI Outreach (75 sec)** — Pick a tone, generate a draft, edit it, and point out whether the source is OpenAI or fallback.
7. **Campaign (45 sec)** — Create a campaign and assign the lead.
8. **Sequence (45 sec)** — Show Day 1, Day 3 and Day 6 steps.
9. **Simulation + Analytics (60 sec)** — Trigger a simulated send/reply/meeting and refresh analytics.
10. **Plans/Payment (60 sec)** — Open Plans, choose Starter or Professional, launch Razorpay test checkout, complete a test payment, and show the new payment record/current plan.
11. **Inbox/Admin (45 sec)** — Categorize a simulated reply, then show Django Admin and the stored Payment/Subscription records.

Closing line: “SignalFlow AI focuses on the gap between knowing who the lead is and knowing why the salesperson should contact that lead now.”
