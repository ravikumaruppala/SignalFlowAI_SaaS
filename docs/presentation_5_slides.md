# 5-Slide Presentation Outline

## Slide 1 — Problem + Target Customer
- Sales teams switch between CRM, prospecting, outreach, follow-up and analytics tools.
- Target: small and mid-sized B2B sales teams, founders, SDRs and sales managers.

## Slide 2 — Solution + Workflow
- Lead → Signal → Reason → Message → Sequence → Result.
- Explainable score + Why Now + personalized outreach + simple sequence + analytics.

## Slide 3 — TAM / SAM / SOM
- TAM: $28.7B worldwide CRM sales software forecast for 2025 (source label in supplied blueprint: Gartner, Oct 7 2025).
- SAM: $3.89B worldwide sales engagement platform software market in 2025 (source label in supplied blueprint: The Insight Partners, Apr 17 2026).
- SOM: internal example target of 100 customers × $200/month = $240k ARR; this is a business assumption, not a measured market size.

## Slide 4 — Business Model + Payments
- Free, Starter, Professional and Business plans.
- Illustrative plan limits and demo pricing are product-design assumptions.
- Starter and Professional use Razorpay Standard Checkout in test mode.
- Payment is verified by Django before the 30-day plan is activated.
- Recurring auto-renewal is future scope via Razorpay Subscriptions.

## Slide 5 — Competition + Differentiation + Roadmap
- Conceptual references: HubSpot, Instantly, Apollo, Clay, Gong.
- Differentiator: the reason-to-contact decision and “Why Now?” explanation.
- Roadmap: enrichment → email sending → recurring billing → CRM sync → multichannel → advanced signals → team/enterprise controls.
